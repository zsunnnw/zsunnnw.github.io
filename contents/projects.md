<p class="category">Single-cell transcriptomics</p>
<h3>Immune cell profiles in pancreatic cancer</h3>
<p>I analyzed 80,209 peripheral blood cells from 17 people with pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma and four healthy controls. Using Seurat, Harmony, and Azimuth, I identified differences in immune-cell gene expression, including zinc-related pathways in natural killer cells.</p>
