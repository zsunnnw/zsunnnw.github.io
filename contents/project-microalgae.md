## Overview

My undergraduate thesis studied which bacteria in the RC1 microalgae–bacteria system supported algal growth and nutrient removal in wastewater.

## Study design

I compared microalgae–bacteria pairings and measured changes in algal growth, nitrogen, phosphorus, and chemical oxygen demand. Pairings showed different patterns of nutrient removal.
